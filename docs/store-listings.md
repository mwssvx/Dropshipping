## Description (App Store 4000 / Play 4000)

Run your dropshipping store from your phone.

Storefront is the app for stores on Storefront. See today's sales the moment they happen, fix orders that need you, and approve what the AI suggests with a tap.

TODAY AT A GLANCE
- Sales and orders for today and the last 7 days
- One list of what needs you: payout holds, problem orders, messages
- Approve or turn down AI suggestions with a swipe

ORDERS THAT SHIP THEMSELVES
- Paid orders go to your supplier on their own
- If a supplier can't take one, see why and fix it: retry, add your own tracking, or refund
- Refund in full or in part from the order

ADD PRODUCTS IN SECONDS
- Paste a supplier link and the product comes in with photos, options and costs
- Let AI write the title and description, then put it on sale
- Change prices and stock, or take a photo for the product

MONEY WITHOUT SURPRISES
- What each sale nets after fees, and when your next payout lands
- If payouts are paused, see the reason and send the document from your camera

AN ASSISTANT THAT ASKS FIRST
- Ask about your store in plain words
- It drafts replies to buyers and fixes for stuck orders, and waits for your OK

NOTIFICATIONS YOU CHOOSE
- New orders (with a sound you can turn off), problem orders, payout holds, margin alerts and replies from our team

Works offline: see what was loaded last, and approvals and price changes you make offline send when you're back online.

You need a Storefront store to use the app. Opening one is free at <domain>.

## App privacy answers (App Store "nutrition label" / Play data safety)

- **Collected and linked to the user:** email address and name (account), user content (product photos the merchant uploads, messages to the assistant), identifiers (push token).
- **Not collected:** location, contacts, browsing history, health, financial info (card details never touch the app; payouts are set up with Stripe on the web).
- **Tracking:** none. No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Data deletion:** merchants can close their account; request link: https://<domain>/legal/privacy.
- **Encryption in transit:** yes (HTTPS only).
